About me

I'm Jervil Angelo De Leon, a BSIT graduate from FEU Institute of Technology, based in Makati. I work in HRIS Technical Support and spend my own time learning cloud computing by building on AWS.

At work I give Level 1 support for an HR information system used by seafarers and office staff: resolving issues, escalating the complex ones, documenting known fixes, and supporting the vendor rollout. That rollout is where my template tracker automation came from.

My path into IT went through animation and game development, a C# programming internship, and onsite hardware work for an HP service centre. Each stage added something I still use: programming, hands-on troubleshooting, and explaining a fix to the person who depends on it.

Cloud is where I'm taking that next. I study for a certification, then build something real with it: a serverless ticketing API modelled on my day job, a VPC and EC2 setup to understand networking and security, and Teolohiya.site, a moderated book platform now live on AWS.

What I'm focused on

  • Serverless architectureLambda, API Gateway, AppSync and DynamoDB, and keeping cost tied to usage.
  • Networking and securityVPCs, security groups, least-privilege IAM roles and Cognito-based access.
  • Operations and costCloudWatch logs and alarms, AWS Budgets, and avoiding always-on spend like NAT gateways.
  • Infrastructure as codeAmplify Gen 2 with CDK and CloudFormation; next, rebuilding the ticketing API as an AWS SAM template.
  • AutomationPower Automate and Power BI at work, to replace manual checks with something that runs every day.

Where I'm heading

A junior cloud, infrastructure or systems role where I can keep building, learn from experienced engineers, and look after systems people rely on.
