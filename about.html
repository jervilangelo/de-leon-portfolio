About me

I’ve loved technology since I was a kid. Studying IT at FEU Institute of Technology, where I specialized in Animation and Game Development, gave me a way to bring creative work and programming together.

I went into hardware troubleshooting and then IT support. These days, I support an HR system used by seafarers and office staff. Working directly with the people who use it has taught me to ask what they’re trying to get done before I start working on a fix.

I like automating the tasks people have to repeat. At work, I built an HRIS template tracker that checks submissions daily and shows the team where to follow up.

I also love books. I built Teolohiya.site to help readers in the Philippines find theological books and contact sellers. Deploying it on AWS gave me a chance to work through how sign-in, photo uploads, and moderation fit together in an app people can use.

Where I’m heading

I’m working toward a role in cloud infrastructure. I learn by deploying projects myself, then figuring out how to keep them running and make them easier to support.
