About me

Hey, I'm Jervil! Game dev, video editor, programmer, helpdesk guy. Basically your all-in-one IT person, based in Makati.

Right now I'm on helpdesk for an HRIS project, supporting the HR system our seafarers and office staff use every day. I fix what I can, escalate what I can't, and write the fixes down so nobody has to solve the same problem twice. That's actually where my template tracker automation came from.

I got here the long way round: a BSIT degree from FEU Tech in animation and game development, a C# programming internship, then fixing hardware onsite for an HP service centre. Every stop taught me something I still use.

Where I'm headed: Cloud Engineer. My routine is simple. Pass a cert, then build something real with it on AWS, like a serverless ticketing API, a locked-down EC2 setup, and Teolohiya.site, which is live right now.
