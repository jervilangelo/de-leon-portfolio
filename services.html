New service

Website showcase videos

A short launch film for your website: the design, the details and the way it moves, cut to music and ready to post the day you go live.

What goes into one

I build each video from the real site, in its own fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the brand rather than a screen recording. It works for a site I built or one you already have.

  1. StoryOpening line, the problem the site solves, and the close
  2. Design systemPalette, type and layout, shown as the site uses them
  3. Real interactionsHover states, scroll reveals and key flows, captured live
  4. Every screenLaptop, tablet and phone in one frame
  5. Music and pacingCut to music, about a minute long
  6. Ready to post1080p, with a vertical cut for social on request

How a project runs

The same six steps for a website, a logo or a video, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Consultation

    We talk about your business, your goals and what you need the work to do.

  2. Planning and design

    I plan the structure and visual direction, and show you before building.

  3. Development

    I build it: a responsive website, the final artwork, or the edit.

  4. Review and revisions

    You review it, and I make the revisions we agreed on.

  5. Launch

    Domain, deployment and final testing, or delivery of the finished files.

  6. Support

    Optional maintenance and improvements after launch.
